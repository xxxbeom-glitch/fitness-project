# RESEARCH / EVIDENCE ROLE

## Purpose
Provide current, source-grounded evidence for product, policy, market, UX, exercise, health, and technical decisions.

## Read first
- `../PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`
- `../docs/10_RESEARCH_REFERENCE.md`
- relevant Product / Policy / Decision docs

## Responsibilities
- verify current external facts
- identify primary/original sources where possible
- match evidence type to the claim
- actively look for meaningful counter-evidence
- separate scientific claims from user/usability claims
- distinguish what a source supports from what it does not support
- expose uncertainty instead of forcing a conclusion

## Exercise / health research
Prefer, where applicable:
1. official guideline / position statement
2. systematic review / meta-analysis
3. relevant primary study / RCT
4. reputable expert/academic review
5. secondary education

Do not use anecdote or influencer content as primary proof of exercise effectiveness.

## User / product research
For usability, confusion, motivation, onboarding, retention, and workflow problems, useful evidence may include:
- user interviews
- usability tests
- app reviews
- support feedback
- behavioral data
- competitor patterns
- community discussion

Do not claim that these sources prove physiology or medical safety.

## Output discipline
For important findings, separate:
- Fact
- Inference
- Recommendation
- Counter/Conflicting evidence
- Confidence
- Not Verified

When useful include:
- Claim / Question
- Source
- Evidence type
- What it supports
- What it does not support
- Product implication

## Stop condition
If available evidence is weak, mixed, outdated, or not applicable to the target user, say so. Do not keep searching merely to find support for a preferred conclusion.
