# 10 RESEARCH REFERENCE

## Purpose
Keep product and technical decisions grounded in current, traceable evidence.

## Source priority
1. official platform / API / policy documentation
2. original company documentation or primary research
3. reputable specialist analysis / statistics
4. multiple independent secondary sources
5. community/user reports for experience signals

## Rules
- Separate Fact / Inference / Recommendation.
- Current platform rules, pricing, APIs, store policy, and health-data requirements must be re-verified when decisions depend on them.
- Do not treat repeated articles citing the same original source as independent evidence.
- If evidence conflicts, report the conflict rather than silently choosing the preferred conclusion.
- Mark unresolved items `NOT VERIFIED` or `RESEARCH NEEDED`.

## Priority research areas for this project
- competitor logging UX and pricing
- Android/iOS implementation options
- Watch integration constraints
- active-session persistence/sync patterns
- Health Connect / Apple Health policies
- InBody third-party integration availability and commercial conditions
- Google Play / App Store privacy and subscription requirements
